    02

    Research

    One result in full, because it is the one worth reading closely. Expected calibration error on 499,995 SDSS DR17 sources, where lower is better.

    Each recalibrator was fitted on bright sources and then applied to the selected magnitude bin, so these three columns are a transfer test. Green marks the best transfer in a row, magenta the worst. At the faint end the capable models tell the clearest story: random forest and MLP both hold under temperature scaling and break badly under Platt.
