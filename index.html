Research

Predicting whether bacteria cause disease

Dr. Rajeev Azad's lab, University of North Texas, since May 2026

I compare PathogenFinder2 and ESM3 protein-language-model embeddings for predicting bacterial pathogenicity from genomes, training and evaluating on TACC's Lonestar6 supercomputer. The work placed 3rd in Computer Science/Mathematics I at the 2026 Texas Junior Academy of Science, and a lab manuscript is in preparation.

Forecasting flu season peaks

Supervised by Dr. Subhro Mitra, University of Texas at Dallas, since May 2026

A leave-one-season-out forecasting pipeline over 22 CDC influenza seasons, with a leakage firewall audited across all 114 model fits. Prophet's 80% prediction intervals covered only 5.9% to 10.5% of outcomes, while a simple cumulative-ILI regression beat the climatology baseline at every lead time. Dr. Mitra now uses the pipeline to teach his students.

Code and results

Deforestation detection across biomes

First author, under review at the CV4EO workshop, WACV 2027

A ResNet50 trained on EuroSAT reaches 98.3% benchmark accuracy, then collapses at 3 of 8 real deforestation frontiers. Label-free AdaBN repairs every collapse, but a simple NDVI baseline still scores the highest mean F1 (0.34 against 0.25 and 0.17). Benchmark accuracy did not transfer for free, and the paper says so.

Code and results

Calibration under magnitude shift

First author, unpublished manuscript

On 499,995 SDSS sources, capable star, galaxy and quasar classifiers stay calibrated as sources grow fainter, but recalibration fit on bright sources breaks on faint ones: Platt scaling raises faint-bin calibration error from 0.004 to 0.063. The chart at the top of this page comes from this study.

A drug screen that found nothing

Independent study, summer 2026

A reproducible docking screen of 77 compounds against SpyCEP, a protease of flesh-eating strep. No candidate stood out, and the pipeline failed its own positive control, so I retracted an earlier headline finding and reported the null result instead.

Vocabulary size in very small language models

Preregistered study, paused at the pilot stage

Does a published law for compute-optimal vocabulary size hold below the range it was fitted on? The plan was approved after seven rounds of adversarial review; there are no results yet.
